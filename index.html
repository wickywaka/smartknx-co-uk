What I do

Every part of a KNX building.

Lighting control

Switching, dimming, DALI, colour temperature and RGB, presence and daylight control, scenes.

Blinds & curtains

Motorised blinds, shutters with slat control, curtains, and sun and wind protection logic.

Heating & HVAC

Room temperature control, underfloor heating, fan coils and ventilation, all from one schedule.

Energy metering

Electricity, water and heat metering brought onto the bus for monitoring and reporting.

Logic & scenes

Logic modules from ABB, Zennio and MDT for the behaviour your client actually asked for.

Integration

KNX connected to the AV, security and building-management systems around it, with one joined-up control.

Security & access

Smart locks, intercoms, cameras and alarm status tied into your KNX scenes and controls.

Audio & entertainment

Multi-room audio, Sonos and home cinema, controlled alongside lighting and blinds from anywhere.

Repairs & maintenance

Faulty switches, dead sensors, lost ETS projects or changed routines: fixed on site at a fair, straightforward rate.
